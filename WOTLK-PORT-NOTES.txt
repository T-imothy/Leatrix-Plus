LEATRIX PLUS - WOTLK 3.3.5A PORT

This build carries the Classic all-in-one feature set into the 3.3.5a client.
It uses explicit Wrath event arguments, Wrath aura fallbacks, a blue dark
interface, legacy-continent flight presets, and learned timing for routes that
are not in the preset data.

Open the options with /ltp or the minimap button.
