Leatrix Plus 1.12.1 all-in-one.16

- Quest automation now waits 0.30 seconds for the NPC dialog to stabilize and
  performs exactly one action from each refreshed quest/gossip state.
- Native quest events and the v14 private-client visible-panel fallback now use
  the same queue, preventing them from racing or reusing stale quest indices.
- Multiple completed and available quests remain supported: every reward or
  refreshed list emits/creates the next queued action.
- Zygor remains a separate passive guide/tracker. Automate quests in LTP is the
  single optional owner of accepting and turning in quests.
